# Fabricated artifact cleanup audit

Date: 2026-08-22

The previous workspace mixed genuine YOLO forward passes with fabricated
fallbacks and presented the combined files as results. Those files were removed
from the active workspace rather than renamed as verified output.

## Removed fake or unverified execution paths

- `scripts/generate_all_notebook_results.py`: drew synthetic classroom frames,
  fixed people/keypoints/boxes, and generated scenario events.
- `scripts/process_all_8_videos.py`: inserted fixed boxes/keypoints when no model
  detection existed.
- `scripts/render_real_cctv_dataset_overlays.py`: inserted calibrated fallback
  boxes, scenario-triggered events, and heuristic SMPL-X overlays.
- `scripts/run_five_videos_real_pipeline.py`: injected fallback detections,
  changed 3D values by video ID, injected events, and fixed VRAM values.
- `scripts/run_notebook_1_execution.py`, `run_notebook_2_execution.py`, and
  `run_notebook_3_benchmark.py`: generated or compared unverified result files,
  including hard-coded runtime/VRAM/benchmark values.
- `scripts/test_pipeline_execution.py`: generated synthetic CCTV and simulated
  events. Real unit tests remain under `tests/` and are labelled as fixtures.
- Legacy root and `drishti4d/notebooks/` notebooks: duplicate/unexecuted paths
  tied to the scripts above or containing claimed comparison numbers.
- `src/drishti/providers/yolo_family_provider.py`: labelled YOLOv8 weights as a
  YOLO26 provider and assigned seats from detection order.
- `src/drishti/providers/rtmpose_provider.py`: swallowed inference failures and
  contained an unimplemented RTMPose result parser.

## Removed fake or unverified data/results

- `outputs/`: mixed generated results, synthetic videos, injected events,
  fixed metrics, and claimed GPU/VRAM measurements. It was untracked and is not
  recoverable from Git.
- `drishti4d/data/annotations/ground_truth_benchmark.json`: unverified temporal
  ground truth used to produce benchmark claims.
- `drishti4d/configs/cameras/*.yaml`: unverified fixed seat/monitor/lap polygons.
- `drishti4d/artifacts/hard_negatives/`: fixed crops and labels generated by the
  fabricated pipeline.
- `drishti4d/data/contact_sheets/` and `drishti_contact_sheets.zip`: prior
  extracted artifacts that were not tied to the five-source manifest.
- Extracted duplicate SMPL-X directories and the 870 MB archive: body-model
  assets were loaded as though they performed image-to-3D inference. They do not.

Tracked deletions remain recoverable from Git history. New results are accepted
only under `outputs/verified/<run-id>/` and must include `run.json` provenance.
